## Dataset Overview

Source: IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset (public)

This dataset has been adapted to approximate SME energy customers.

### Key Variables (Reframed)
- tenure → customer relationship duration
- MonthlyCharges → average monthly energy bill
- Contract → energy contract type
- PaymentMethod → billing method
- Churn → customer exit indicator

### Rationale for Use
While originally from the telecom sector, the dataset captures
contractual, pricing, and tenure dynamics similar to SME energy providers.

The focus of this project is on analytical reasoning and insight generation,
not on industry-specific raw data.
